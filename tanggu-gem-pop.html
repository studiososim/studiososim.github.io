Privacy Policy

Effective: September 4, 2026

Studio Sosim ("we") respects the privacy of Tanggu Gem Pop users. This policy explains what the app processes and the choices available to users.

1. Information we process

  • If you choose passwordless email-link login: email address, Supabase user ID, nickname, Classic best score, ranking, and update time. We do not collect or store a password.
  • If you play as a guest: a random anonymous identifier, nickname, and Classic best score.
  • If you choose Game Center on iOS: the app-specific Game Center player identifier, verification information, and connection status.
  • We do not ask you to enter a phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. On-device information

The board, current score, best records, combo, hearts and recharge time, language, sounds, theme, and settings are stored on the device. Advance Mode starts at Stage 1 for each attempt; its progress and scores stay on the device and are not included in online rankings.

3. Accounts, Supabase, and rankings

Supabase provides automatic guest authentication, passwordless email-link login, Game Center account linking, the Classic leaderboard, and account deletion. Email login is completed by opening the Studio Sosim webpage from the verification email and tapping “Open Tanggu Gem Pop.” Connecting email or Game Center merges the current guest nickname and Classic best score into that account and keeps the higher score. The login session remains on the device until the user explicitly signs out or deletes the account. Signing out affects only this game and creates a fresh guest. Tanggu Gem Pop accounts are separate from accounts for other Studio Sosim games.

Supabase may process IP address, device/app information, access logs, and security or diagnostic information as a service provider. Data may be processed outside your country.

4. Advertising, payments, and tracking

This version contains no advertising SDK, shows no advertisements, has no in-app purchases, and does not use data to track users across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Purposes and retention

Information is used for app functionality, account security, fraud prevention, Classic score submission, live rankings, data synchronization, troubleshooting, and service stability. Server data is retained while the account exists. Local data generally remains until the app is removed, subject to device backup settings. We do not sell personal information.

6. Account deletion

In Settings, choose Delete Account to permanently delete the Tanggu Gem Pop account, email account where applicable, nickname, Classic score, ranking record, stored progress, and Game Center link. Deletion cannot be undone.

For assistance, email studiososim@gmail.com. We may request minimal information needed to verify the request.

7. Children, security, and rights

The app does not ask users to enter a real name, phone number, address, contacts, photos, or precise location. Parents or guardians should supervise children's account choices. We use HTTPS, access controls, and data minimization. Users start as a guest automatically and may change their nickname, use passwordless email-link login or Game Center, sign out, or delete their account in the app.

8. Services, AI-assisted graphics, and contact

Supabase is used for authentication and the Classic leaderboard. Apple Game Center is optional on iOS. Tanggu is an original Studio Sosim character. Some graphics were created or refined with AI-assisted tools and then selected and edited by Studio Sosim. Open-source and font notices are available inside the app.
