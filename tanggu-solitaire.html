Privacy Policy

Effective: September 30, 2026

1. Personal data collected

The app has no account or login. Studio Sosim does not directly collect names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. On-device storage

Language, card back, board color, hearts, and recharge time stay only in local device storage and are not sent to an external server. They may be removed when the app is deleted or device data is cleared.

3. Network and third-party services

Optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob and Google UMP. Google may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Consent is checked before ad requests, and privacy choices are available where required. Gameplay works offline; ads require an internet connection. Hearts refill by one every 30 minutes up to five. Completing a rewarded ad grants one heart. There are no banner ads. Uninstall the app to delete local game data. Contact studiososim@gmail.com for deletion questions. You can manage ad privacy choices in device settings. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID; on iOS, see Privacy & Security settings.
